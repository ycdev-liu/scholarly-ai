# Literature Review

1. State the research question and group papers by approach, not by search-result order.
2. Compare methods, findings, limitations, and disagreements across sources.
3. Cite numbered evidence entries for every substantive claim.
4. Explain coverage gaps when the available papers are few or narrowly scoped.
